## What is Spinr?

Spinr turns the product photos a store already has (1–4 angles) into an
interactive 360° spin, directly on the product page. The spin is
generated by AI in a few minutes. No photography rig, no 3D modeling,
no code required.

Important: a Spinr spin is NOT just a rotating video. It is fully
INTERACTIVE — shoppers grab the product and turn it themselves, with a
mouse on desktop or a finger on mobile. They control the rotation:
spin it, stop it, turn it back, inspect any angle at their own pace.
When idle, the spin rotates on its own; the moment a shopper touches
it, they're in control.

Merchants can also DOWNLOAD every spin as an MP4 video file — useful
for social media posts, ads, marketplaces, or anywhere a regular video
is needed. The download is included on every plan.

## Who is Spinr for? (Shopify AND everyone else)

Spinr serves two audiences:

1. **Shopify merchants** — the Spinr Shopify app gives the full
   integrated experience: pick a product from your catalog, one-click
   push to the product page, billing through your Shopify invoice.
2. **Global / non-Shopify users** — any store or website worldwide can
   use Spinr through thespinr.com: upload photos, get the spin, and
   paste a one-line embed that works on WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace,
   Webflow, BigCommerce, and custom-built sites. If a visitor asks
   "does Spinr work with [any platform]?" the answer is yes wherever a
   line of HTML can be added to a page.

Never tell a non-Shopify user that Spinr is Shopify-only — it isn't.

## How it works

1. Pick a product (Shopify app) or upload 1–4 photos (thespinr.com).
2. Spinr removes the photo backgrounds automatically and generates a
   smooth 360° rotation with studio-style lighting.
3. Push the spin to your product page in one click (Shopify), or paste a
   one-line embed on any other website.

## Photo guidelines (most common support topic)

- 1–4 photos of the product are accepted — and MORE PHOTOS = BETTER
  RESULTS. With 4 photos (front, back, and both sides) the AI grounds
  every angle in a real photo, giving the best and most accurate spin.
  With only 1 photo the spin still works, but the AI has to imagine the
  unseen sides — expect a noticeably less accurate result. Always
  recommend uploading as many angles as the user has, up to 4.
- Ordinary catalog photos or phone photos are fine.
- Even lighting and roughly the same distance for every angle.
- Any background is fine — it's removed automatically.
- One product per photo: if photos show a pair (e.g. both shoes), the
  spin renders a single item.
- In the Shopify app, Spinr uses the first 4 images on the product, in
  their gallery order (1st = front, then back, left, right).

## Shopify app usage

- Install from the Shopify App Store; the app opens inside the Shopify
  admin. No separate account or password is needed.
- Click "Create spin" on any product with at least one photo. A progress
  bar shows generation status — usually a few minutes; the page updates
  itself.
- When the spin is ready, click "Push to page" to put it on the product
  page.
- One-time theme setup: click "Add the Spinr block" in the app — it opens
  the Theme Editor with the "Spinr 360° spin" block added to the product
  template; click Save. This is needed once per theme; after that every
  pushed spin appears automatically on its product page. Products
  without a spin are unaffected.
- Not happy with a result? Click "Regenerate" on the product row (it
  asks for confirmation because a regeneration uses one spin credit).
- While a spin regenerates, the existing spin stays live on the
  storefront — it is only replaced when the new one finishes
  successfully.

## Using Spinr WITHOUT Shopify

- Create spins at thespinr.com (sign in with Google), then copy the
  embed snippet from the result page. It is one line of HTML (a div plus
  a script tag) that works on any website that allows embeds:
  WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, custom sites.

## Troubleshooting

- "Create spin" is disabled → the product has no photos on Shopify. Add
  at least one photo to the product first.
- The spin doesn't appear on my product page → make sure you clicked
  "Push to page" AND completed the one-time theme setup ("Add the Spinr
  block", then Save in the Theme Editor).
- The spin looks wrong (spins the wrong way, odd artifacts) → click
  Regenerate. AI generation varies between runs; one retry usually
  fixes it. Regeneration uses one spin credit.
- The app shows "reopen the app in a few seconds" → temporary setup
  delay on a fresh install; reopening the app resolves it.
- The spin briefly shows an error on very first load of the day → the
  page retries automatically; reload if needed.
- My product photos show a pair but I want one item → that's automatic;
  the spin renders a single item. If it doesn't, regenerate.
